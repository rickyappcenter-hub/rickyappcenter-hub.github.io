TripNest

A private family trip planner.

TripNest is a personal, self-hosted application for organising family travel — bookings, itineraries and shared expenses. It runs on a single computer in the owner's home and is not a public service. There is nothing here to sign up for.

It optionally connects to the owner's own Google account to read booking confirmation emails and to file booking documents into their own Google Drive.
