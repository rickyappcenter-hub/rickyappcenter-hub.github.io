TripNest — Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

What TripNest is

TripNest is a private, self-hosted trip planner used by one household. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no user accounts beyond those the owner creates on their own device.

Where data is stored

All trip data — bookings, itineraries, expenses, traveller names — is stored in a file on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer, because there isn't one.

Google account access

With the owner's explicit authorisation, TripNest may use:

  • Google Drive (drive.file) — limited by Google to files TripNest itself creates. It cannot see or modify anything else in Drive. Used only to file booking documents the owner chooses to save.
  • Gmail, read over IMAP with an app password, to find booking confirmations. The mailbox is opened read-only; TripNest cannot mark messages read, change labels, or delete anything.

Google account data is never sold, published, or shared with third parties.

Automated analysis of booking emails

When the owner asks TripNest to extract details from a booking email, the text of that email may be sent to a language model for analysis. Depending on configuration this is either a model running on the owner's own hardware, in which case nothing leaves the household network, or Anthropic's Claude service. Images such as receipts and barcodes are only ever processed by the local model. No analysis happens unless the owner initiates it.

Retention and removal

Data persists until the owner deletes it. Google access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings, which immediately ends TripNest's ability to read mail or write to Drive.
